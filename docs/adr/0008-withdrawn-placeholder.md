# ADR 0008: Withdrawn (Number Reserved, Not Published)

**Status:** Withdrawn
**Date:** N/A (number reserved; document never published)
**Context:** ADR numbers 0007 and 0009 are both in use. ADR-0008 was reserved during
the v0.6.0–v0.9.x development period but the corresponding design record was never
authored or published. The gap was first noted informally in maint-2026-07-08
(doc-drift-findings.md, as an aside while documenting ADR-012's absence) and formally
recorded as finding DD-004 in maint-2026-07-09 (doc-drift.md). No operational impact.

## Decision

This file is a permanent placeholder explaining the numbering gap. ADR-0008 was never
assigned to a specific design decision. The next ADR authored after 0007 was 0009
(pcapng reader design, v0.9.3). The gap is expected, not an oversight.

Note: the risk-assumption-monitoring register references "ADR-008" informally in
association with ARP binding-table design assumptions (ASM-CAND-006). This refers to
internal ARP analyzer design notes, not a formally published ADR-0008 document.

## Consequences

None. ADRs 0001–0007 and 0009–0012 remain in force. Future ADRs should continue the
sequence from 0013 onward.
